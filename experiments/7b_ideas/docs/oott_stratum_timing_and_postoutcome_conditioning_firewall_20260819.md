# OOTT stratum timing and post-outcome-conditioning firewall

Every OOTT stratum needs a frozen, hashed manifest before analysis. Provenance—not the variable name—mechanically assigns it to P, O, or Y.

- **P** is genuinely frozen before the compared policy and outcome. Only P may support preregistered heterogeneity/effect-modifier screening and O3a paired transport.
- **O** is induced by policy output, including generated memory length, cap binding, state-hash novelty, writer stop/parser route, or candidate validity. O is restricted to O3b distribution shift, common support, coverage, and selected descriptive concordance. Every O report must include the unstratified total contrast. Causal-effect, confounder, mediator, and “occupancy caused degradation” language is forbidden.
- **Y** depends on the final response, gold, or reward: EM/F1 exact/partial/wrong, reward win/loss, final parser, and semantic outcome. Y is routed only to RED calibration/error analysis and cannot support occupancy localization.

O3 is therefore split into O3a (P-only pre-outcome transport heterogeneity) and O3b (O-only induced occupancy support map). Unknown provenance, O requests for causal/mediator claims, Y requests for occupancy claims, missing manifests, and O reports without the total unstratified contrast hard fail. This firewall adds no rollout or training and never changes method eligibility or the frozen completion-to-200 rule.
