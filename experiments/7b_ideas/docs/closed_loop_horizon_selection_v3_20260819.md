# Closed-loop horizon-selection v3

Closed-loop execution remains unauthorized. The recommended future mode is `H_fixed`: before any Confirm32 policy outcome, choose horizon 2 or 3 using only plumbing, resources, failure behavior, and outcome-blind claim need, then execute that complete frozen horizon once. Audit16 may not select horizon from policy-value direction.

`H_selected` means the third turn is triggered after observing the same Confirm32 two-turn outcome. The prefrozen two-turn adjudication remains primary. Turn three is only an `outcome-triggered selected-horizon stress description`: ordinary unselected intervals or p-values are forbidden, it cannot become a three-turn confirmation, and a positive three-turn view cannot replace a negative two-turn result. The maximum output is `FINITE_TWO_TURN_ACTIONABILITY_WITH_SELECTED_THREE_TURN_DESCRIPTION`.

A confirmatory three-turn study would require untouched examples with horizon, policies, and thresholds frozen in advance; this suite authorizes no such additional resources. The exact identity `Z2=Z3~N(0,1)`, observing Z3 only when Z2>0, has zero true effects but conditional mean `sqrt(2/pi)=0.79788456`, demonstrating the selection bias.
